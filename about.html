Our Story – From a Dream to a Destination

What began in 1998 as a small family-owned retreat along the serene coast of Kerala has grown into Infinite Getaways (InfGet), a hospitality brand built around the belief that travel should be more than just a change of scenery. Founded by a family passionate about travel, culture, and exceptional hospitality, InfGet set out to create places where guests could feel at home while discovering something extraordinary. Over the years, that simple vision expanded beyond borders, bringing together a collection of distinctive resorts, each inspired by its surroundings and local heritage. From peaceful coastal escapes to luxurious mountain retreats, every InfGet destination reflects our commitment to thoughtful experiences, genuine hospitality, and unforgettable memories. Today, InfGet continues to grow with the same spirit with which it began: creating destinations that bring people together, celebrate cultures, and turn every getaway into a story worth telling.

Our Vision

To become a globally admired hospitality brand, redefining the art of travel through exceptional destinations, meaningful experiences, and a commitment to making every journey unforgettable.

Our Mission

At Infinite Getaways, our mission is to create extraordinary stays that go beyond expectations. We strive to deliver personalised hospitality, celebrate the cultures and beauty of every destination, and provide thoughtfully designed spaces where guests can relax, explore, connect, and create lasting memories. Through innovation, sustainability, and genuine care, we aim to make every getaway truly special.
